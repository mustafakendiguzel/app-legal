Privacy Policy — StayLedger

_Last updated: 2026-07-10_

StayLedger ("the app") is built privacy-first.

What we collect

  • Your entries (amounts, categories, notes) are stored locally on your device by default.
    • We have no server access to them. <!-- DATA_BACKEND=convex ise: "are stored on our secure backend (Convex) solely to sync your data across your devices." -->
  • No account is required. We collect no name, email, or contacts.
  • No bank linking. You enter data manually; we never connect to financial institutions.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat (our subscription manager). RevenueCat receives
    • an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts — never your identity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • No tracking. We use no advertising SDKs and do not track you across apps or websites.

Data retention & deletion

Local data lives on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. <!-- convex: "You can erase synced data from Settings → Delete my data." -->
