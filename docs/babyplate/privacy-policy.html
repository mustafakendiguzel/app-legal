Privacy Policy — BabyPlate

Last updated: September 9, 2026

BabyPlate ("the App") helps parents track their baby's first foods, reactions and allergen introductions. This policy explains what the App does with information. Short version: your data stays on your device; we do not run accounts, analytics or advertising.

Who we are

BabyPlate is published by Mustafa Kendigüzel (Qora), Türkiye. Contact: support@qorai.app.

Information you enter

When you use the App you may enter your baby's name, birth date, feeding approach, dietary framework, family allergy history, and food logs (foods tried, reactions, symptoms, notes). This information is stored only on your device in the App's private storage. It is not uploaded to us and we cannot see it. Deleting the App, or using Profile › Reset app, removes it.

Information processed for subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store. To validate purchases and unlock features, the App uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives: an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, the App Store purchase receipt, your device model and OS version, and the App's version. RevenueCat does not receive your baby's name, dates or food logs. Apple processes your payment under its own privacy policy. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or collect e-mail addresses.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, tracking pixels or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics.

Children

The App is designed for parents and caregivers, not for use by children. The baby-related details you enter are stored locally on your device only.

Sharing you initiate

The "Share progress" feature creates a text summary that you can send through the iOS share sheet to anyone you choose (for example a caregiver or pediatrician). We do not receive a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Because data lives on your device, you control retention. Uninstall the App or use Profile › Reset app to delete everything. Subscription records held by Apple and RevenueCat follow their retention policies; to request deletion of RevenueCat records, e-mail support@qorai.app with the request and we will process it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete information. Since we hold no personal data about you beyond subscription validation records, requests can be sent to support@qorai.app.

Changes

We will post updates to this page and update the date above.
