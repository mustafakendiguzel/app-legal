Frequently asked questions

Is BabyPlate free?
You can download it for free and see the plan. Full access — the 80+ food guides, the weekly plan, the allergen tracker and the reaction log — requires BabyPlate Pro. The yearly plan includes a free trial.

How do I cancel?
iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › BabyPlate › Cancel. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

I bought Pro on another device.
Open the App › Profile › Restore purchases (or the link on the paywall). Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

Where is my data stored?
On your device only. There is no account. Deleting the App deletes your data.

Can I share progress with a caregiver or doctor?
Profile › Share with caregivers or pediatrician creates a text summary you can send anywhere.

Something looks wrong with a food guide.
E-mail us the food name and what you'd change — we review every report with our sources.
